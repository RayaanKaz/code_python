♫  Who I Am

Rayaan

Rayaan is an independent artist making music that lives in the uncomfortable space between feeling too much and not knowing how to say it. The kind of music you put on at 2am when you need someone to understand.

Growing up surrounded by sound from all corners of the world, Rayaan built a sonic vocabulary that borrows from everywhere and sounds like nowhere else. Every lyric is earned. Every production choice is intentional.

Debuting in 2025 with Lovesick — the first entry in a catalog that's already stacking up. Independent, unfiltered, and just getting started.

Who I Am

Rayaan

Rayaan is an independent artist crafting music that lives in the space between vulnerability and confidence — late night feelings put into sound.

Growing up surrounded by music from all corners of the world, Rayaan developed a sound that blends raw emotion with carefully crafted production. Every lyric is personal, every drop is intentional.

Debuting in 2025 with the single Lovesick, Rayaan is just getting started. The sound is evolving, the catalog is growing, and there's a lot more to come.
